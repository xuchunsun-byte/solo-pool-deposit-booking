FAQ

Is this another Jobber or Housecall Pro?

No. No dispatch, quotes, multi-tech routing, or full CRM. It’s a deposit booking page plus calendar and email reminders.

Who takes the deposit?

You do—through your own Stripe account (Connect / Checkout). We bill your SaaS subscription separately via Creem. We do not hold customer funds.

What about refunds and chargebacks on deposits?

Handled in your Stripe under your cancel/reschedule policy. We include a plain-English policy template you can edit; you’re the merchant of record for deposits.

Do you find me customers?

No. This is not lead gen. Share the link with people who already inquire.

SMS reminders?

Email confirm + T-24h email ship first. SMS is a later paid tier if demand shows up.

Can I use this for pest / other trades?

Pool is the first vertical. Pest (first-visit deposit) is a possible expansion after pool validates—not promised on day one.

Annual pricing?

When offered, annual can land around $19–24/mo equivalent. Monthly list is $29.

Refunds on the SaaS subscription?

Final policy on Creem checkout. Suggested baseline: cancel anytime; no refund on partial months unless required by Creem/your policy.
